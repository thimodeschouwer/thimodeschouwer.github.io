I am an analyst in the Analysis and Statistics unit at the Directorate General for Employment, Social Affairs, and Inclusion (EMPL) of the [European Commission](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/index_en), where I build models to inform employment policy and study the labor market impacts of AI. Alongside, I am an affiliated researcher at the Economics Department of the [University of Antwerp](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/research-groups/economics/). I use search and matching models to study the labor market, exploring questions such as how workers and firms value different aspects of jobs or what motivates people to adopt AI at the workplace.

I hold a [PhD]({{ site.doctoral_thesis_link }}) in Economics from [KU Leuven](https://feb.kuleuven.be/research/economics), funded by a Research Fellowship from the [Research Foundation Flanders](https://www.fwo.be/en/) (FWO), which included a research visit at the Institute for Applied Microeconomics (IAME) at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/university/university). Further information can be found on my [CV]({{ site.cv_link }}).
