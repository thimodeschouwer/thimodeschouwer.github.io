## About Me

I am an Economic Analyst in the Directorate General for  [Employment, Social Affairs, and Inclusion (DG EMPL)](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/index_en) at the European Commission. Before, I was a PhD student in the [Economics Department](https://feb.kuleuven.be/research/economics) at KU Leuven, funded by a Fellowship from the [Research Foundation Flanders](https://www.fwo.be/en/). I also spent some time visiting the Institute for Applied Microeconomics (IAME) at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/university/university).

My research focuses on how workers and firms perceive and value non-monetary job attributes. I am interested in what shapes preferences and production technologies, how equilibrium outcomes reflect these primitives, and how the structure of the labor market determines whose interests are reflected in labor market outcomes. To answer these questions, I combine structural models of search and matching with causal inference methods and survey evidence.
