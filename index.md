## {{ site.title }}

I work as an Economic Analyst in the Analysis and Statistics unit of the Directorate General for [Employment, Social Affairs, and Inclusion](https://employment-social-affairs.ec.europa.eu/index_en) at the European Commission, where I study the labor market impacts of AI and build models to inform employment policy. In addition to that, I am an Affiliated Researcher at the Economics Department at the [University of Antwerp](https://www.uantwerpen.be/en/research-groups/economics/). My academic work uses models of search and matching to study labor and marriage markets. I address questions related to how workers and firms value different aspects of jobs, how market conditions shape equilibrium outcomes, or how technological changes affect how matches are formed. I am also interested the adoption of AI in the workplace.

I hold a PhD in Economics from [KU Leuven](https://feb.kuleuven.be/research/economics), funded by a Research Fellowship from the [Research Foundation Flanders](https://www.fwo.be/en/) (FWO), and spent some time visiting the Institute for Applied Microeconomics (IAME) at the [University of Bonn](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/university/university). Further information can be found on my [CV]({{ site.cv_link }}).
